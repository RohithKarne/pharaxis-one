Pharaxis was founded on the belief that medical affairs teams deserve purpose-built software — not repurposed CRM tools or fragmented point solutions.

Pharaxis-One is our answer: a coherent platform that brings MIMS, CP Portal, AI-Agent, Vault, QMS, Safety, IEG, and Publications under unified operating standards, shared data contracts, and a single operational footprint.

We work exclusively with enterprise life science organisations that demand auditability, scalability, and deep clinical workflow alignment.
